Place a legally obtained UTF-8 plain text edition of Henryk Sienkiewicz's
*W pustyni i w puszczy* in this directory as `w_pustyni_i_w_puszczy.txt`.
The original novel (1911) is public domain in Poland. Modern editorial
additions and digitization-specific terms may differ. The corpus is not
included in this release; record its source, edition and SHA-256 before
redistributing or reporting reproducible experiments.
